Earth & open source

Earth textures © Solar System Scope, licensed CC BY 4.0. Based on NASA imagery; resized, re-encoded and (for the relief/ocean-mask map) channel-repacked as 1K/2K/4K WebP by the Watched project by Armaan Gupta. Midnight uses these modified maps without further changes; the Canvas fallback resamples them at runtime.

Earth, cloud and atmosphere shaders, and solar geometry, are adapted directly from Watched (dev-Armaan/satellites, commit 21331c8f), © 2026 Armaan Gupta, MIT license. Midnight retains the upstream rendering formulas; imagery-painting uniforms are disabled. Its application adapter, letter markers and Canvas fallback are separate. No satellite tracking service or Watched branding is included.

Three.js r160 © 2010–2023 Three.js authors, MIT license. Vendored locally; no CDN requests are needed for the globe.

Fonts: Noto Sans and Noto Serif, SIL Open Font License. The street map uses MapLibre / OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap; its attribution remains visible in that view.

Continuous globe-to-street map

MapLibre GL JS 5.6.2, BSD-3-Clause and bundled third-party notices. Street maps by OpenFreeMap, using © OpenStreetMap contributors. Attribution remains visible in the map.

Midnight reprojects the Solar System Scope day map to Web Mercator and composites the static cloud map into 21 local overview tiles. These modified images remain CC BY 4.0. They fade to vector streets when zooming close; they are not high-resolution satellite imagery or live weather.
